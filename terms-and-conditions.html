When you reserve a table at 181 Mercer Street or schedule a private dining experience, you agree to supply authentic, precise contact and guest count details. Table availability is released thirty calendar days in advance on a rolling basis to ensure fair, open access for all culinary enthusiasts. A non-refundable reservation guarantee deposit of fifty dollars per guest is required at the time of booking to secure your table reservation. This deposit is credited in full against your final dining bill upon the conclusion of your meal at our salon.

Because our seasonal ingredients are harvested fresh from local biodynamic farms specifically to match anticipated nightly covers, short-notice cancellations inflict significant food waste and economic disruption. You may cancel or reschedule your reservation with a full refund of your deposit up to forty-eight hours prior to your scheduled dining time.
