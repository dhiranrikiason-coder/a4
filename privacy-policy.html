Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 15, 2026 Last Updated: March 2, 2026 Entity: CorianderFare Mercer Dining Salon LLC

1. Introduction and Data Protection Principles

At CorianderFare, operating our celebrated culinary salon at 181 Mercer Street in New York, we believe that guest privacy constitutes the foundational bedrock of honorable hospitality and bespoke epicurean experiences. This Privacy Policy outlines with thorough precision how our establishment collects, secures, encrypts, and processes personal data submitted through our digital reservation interfaces and dining inquiries. When you reserve a table for our seasonal tasting menu, inquire about botanical ingredients, or register for our culinary research dispatches, you entrust us with personal biographical and dietary information. We pledge unwavering stewardship over this sensitive information, adhering strictly to global privacy frameworks including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act. Every architectural component of our website is engineered to protect guest confidentiality while facilitating seamless, dignified hospitality.

Our commitment to computational confidentiality mirrors our culinary obsession with pure, chemical-free heirloom coriander cultivation and pristine culinary execution. We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal records to third-party data brokers or marketing conglomerates under any circumstances. All digital transmissions executed across corianderfare.com are protected by cutting-edge cryptographic security protocols, ensuring that your reservation details and dietary preferences remain strictly private between our dining desk and your personal device. By interacting with our digital domain, you acknowledge the ethical operational guidelines and data processing workflows detailed throughout this policy document. Should you require updates, modifications, or complete expungement of your archived interaction histories, our guest relations desk remains perpetually at your disposal.

2. Categories of Information Collected from Dining Guests

To furnish flawless dining experiences and honor complex individual dietary needs, CorianderFare collects specific categories of personal information submitted voluntarily by prospective guests. When you initiate a table reservation request or submit private salon inquiries, we record your legal name, telephone contact number, primary electronic mail address, and preferred booking chronologies. Furthermore, dining patrons often provide crucial medical allergen notifications, dietary lifestyle preferences (such as plant-forward or allium-sensitive requirements), and seating accessibility accommodations. These dietary parameters are utilized exclusively by our culinary brigade and front-of-house maître d’ to ensure that every course served across our botanical tasting journey is safe, nourishing, and deeply delightful. We treat your dietary and health-adjacent requests with the highest degree of organizational care and administrative discretion.

In addition to voluntarily provided guest profiles, our web servers automatically log technical metadata essential to sustaining high-performance network reliability and platform responsiveness. This operational telemetry encompasses your originating Internet Protocol address, operating browser engine, device hardware classification, referring uniform resource locators, and localized interaction timestamps. We deploy this aggregate data solely to diagnose server latency, counteract automated denial-of-service attempts, optimize layout rendering across mobile smartphone viewports, and audit regional server load. None of this automated operational telemetry is ever merged with your personal dining profiles or reservation records without your explicit affirmative consent. Our technical telemetry adheres strictly to data minimization standards, ensuring no unnecessary digital footprints linger across our systems.

3. Lawful Grounds and Operational Purposes of Processing

CorianderFare processes personal guest data exclusively under recognized lawful justifications recognized by international data governance regulations, primarily contractual necessity, legitimate operational interest, and affirmative consent. When you reserve a seat for our seasonal tasting menu, processing your contact details and dining party size is contractually necessary to reserve physical table capacity and procure specific farm ingredients. Without these critical data attributes, our culinary salon cannot fulfill its hospitality obligations or prepare courses aligned with your party’s schedule. Furthermore, maintaining archived records of previous visits allows our culinary team to recall your favored botanical infusions and table preferences during subsequent seasonal reservations. Every operational workflow within our company is directly tied to enhancing the culinary value and personalized attentiveness delivered to our guests.

Under our legitimate business interests, we evaluate aggregate website navigation trends to refine our digital botanical menus, enhance mobile legibility, and prevent duplicate automated reservations from depleting table availability. When you subscribe to our botanical gastronomy monographs or seasonal harvest announcements, we rely upon your affirmative consent to deliver periodic editorial content directly to your electronic inbox. You retain the absolute prerogative to revoke this consent at any moment via automated unsubscription links or direct written notice to our concierge team. We do not engage in automated behavioral profiling, artificial intelligence predictive credit scoring, or invasive algorithmic surveillance across any branch of our business. Your data remains an instrument of hospitality excellence, never a commodity for commercial exploitation.

4. Data Retention Protocols and Cryptographic Safeguards

We preserve personal reservation dossiers exclusively for durations strictly commensurate with fulfilling the culinary, legal, fiscal, and operational purposes for which the information was originally captured. Guest dining history profiles and dietary preference logs are retained for a default duration of twenty-four months from your last visit to facilitate personalized hospitality during subsequent seasonal return visits. Conversely, transient customer service inquiries, general menu questions, and unconfirmed reservation submissions are purged systematically after twelve months of communicative inactivity. Financial accounting documentation associated with completed dining transactions is retained in accordance with statutory internal revenue regulations and municipal corporate audit obligations. Once these statutory and operational milestones elapse, physical records are shredded and digital entries undergo irreversible cryptographic sanitization.

The cryptographic infrastructure safeguarding CorianderFare server systems incorporates military-grade Transport Layer Security 1.3 protocol standards for all data traversing transit channels between your browser and our servers. Data stored at rest is encrypted utilizing advanced AES-256 bit encryption algorithms within tier-four sovereign data facilities featuring biometric physical access controls and continuous perimeter surveillance. Our internal team operates under strict role-based access permissions, ensuring that only licensed hospitality staff, reservation managers, and compliance officers can access individual guest dietary portfolios. Regular third-party penetration assessments, vulnerability audits, and administrative security reviews are conducted quarterly to insulate our network from evolving digital risks. In the exceedingly improbable event of a data compromise, affected guests and supervisory authorities will be notified within seventy-two hours in full regulatory alignment.

5. Third-Party Service Providers and Transnational Governance

CorianderFare maintains an unyielding non-disclosure policy, repudiating any sale, lease, or exchange of your personal information to third-party data aggregators or external advertising syndicates. We share confidential guest information exclusively with vetted technical partners whose contributions are indispensable to delivering our restaurant reservation and operational services. These essential partners include PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing gateways for transactional settlements and secure cloud reservation hosting infrastructures. Every third-party contractor is legally bound by executed Data Processing Agreements mandating confidentiality standards no less protective than those upheld within our own Mercer Street dining room. These external partners are strictly prohibited from utilizing guest data for independent commercial ventures or retaining records beyond their immediate task completion.

Because our patrons reside across international capitals, your data may occasionally traverse national borders between the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, and Canada during table reservations and payment verifications. Whenever cross-border data transfers occur, CorianderFare implements Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, along with supplementary technical controls to guarantee an equivalent degree of data security. We rigorously evaluate the legal frameworks of recipient sovereign jurisdictions to verify that foreign surveillance risks remain thoroughly mitigated before initializing electronic transmission. If you reside in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you may request formal copies of these safeguarding contractual instruments by contacting our data protection officer. We remain fully responsible for the continued protection and dignity of your personal information across all international transit corridors.

6. Guest Entitlements, Data Portability, and Erasure Protocols

Applicable international privacy legislation confers comprehensive sovereign entitlements upon guests regarding the custody and governance of their personal records held within our archives. You maintain the undeniable right to demand complete disclosure regarding what specific categories of personal data we retain, the origins of that information, and the exact parties with whom it has been shared. Furthermore, should you discover inaccuracies within your contact records, billing details, or dining preference dossiers, you possess the full legal authority to compel prompt rectification of those records. Where processing is predicated upon consent or legitimate interest, you may lodge formal objections to continued data usage or instruct our administrative desk to impose strict processing restrictions. We honor every verified subject access request without undue delay and without charging arbitrary administrative fees.

Under the statutory provisions of the GDPR 'Right to be Forgotten' and CCPA deletion mandates, you may instruct CorianderFare to permanently erase your personal guest profile from our operational databases. Upon validating your legal identity, we will scrub all non-statutory records across our reservation systems, dietary preference logs, and communication threads, instructing our third-party infrastructure processors to execute identical purges. Guests exercising their statutory privacy rights will never experience discriminatory pricing, diminished hospitality attentiveness, or denied table reservations as a consequence of their decision. To initiate an official data access, rectification, portability, or erasure inquiry, submit a written declaration directly to our compliance desk at care@corianderfare.com or call +1-888-777-5845. We will acknowledge your submission within twenty-four hours and deliver a definitive resolution within thirty calendar days.
